2 · Alignment correction

Hand-held pairs are almost never level: the camera rises, tilts and moves closer between the two shots. Vertical offset and rotation are the parts your eyes cannot fuse — fix them here.

Splitting halves the resampling of either view, so neither eye looks softer than the other.

3 · Depth

Horizontal separation sets where the screen plane sits in the scene. Slide right and the scene recedes behind the screen; slide left and it pushes out towards you.

Comfort rule of thumb: keep the on-screen separation of the farthest object under ~2.5% of image width (viewers cannot diverge their eyes), and pop-out under ~1.5% unless it stays clear of the frame edges.
